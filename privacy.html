Privacy Policy

Effective: June 12, 2026

DonutShorts is a personal content-publishing tool that posts our own gaming videos to TikTok accounts owned and operated by us, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Data we collect

The app stores OAuth access tokens for our own TikTok accounts on our own computer, solely to upload our own videos. We do not collect, process, or share any personal data of other users or third parties.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

Data deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time from TikTok account settings; local tokens are deleted on request.
